- 记忆按「星系」归类，带重要度和遗忘曲线：常用的越记越牢，久不用的慢慢淡下去，仍能搜到（da640949c、bfc838a3c）。
- 约束类和每轮常驻的记忆永不变淡（c22dd9495）。
- 设置里的记忆页改成三栏：左边分类，中间带搜索的列表，右边详情，可改类型、星系、重要度（d0289a4d0）。
- 刚记住的记忆放在「新的」里，一键「对 / 忘掉」；忘掉是归档，不是删除（d0289a4d0）。

## New

- Memories are grouped into galaxies with importance and a forgetting curve (da640949c, bfc838a3c).
- What gets used stays clear; what does not fades slowly and stays searchable (bfc838a3c).
- Constraints and pinned memories never fade (c22dd9495).
- The memory page in Settings has three columns: categories, a searchable list, and details (d0289a4d0).
- Type, galaxy and importance can be changed from the details pane (d0289a4d0).
- Newly learned facts wait under New for a one-click confirm or forget; forget archives (d0289a4d0).
