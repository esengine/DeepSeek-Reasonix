description: Control context growth through concise notes, deduplication, and progressive reading.
category: 基础与元技能
source-url: https://github.com/phoenixlucky/zerotoken-skill
---

Keep working context compact: retain decisions and constraints, remove repeated detail, read only the files needed for the next step, and summarize completed work with evidence and open questions. Do not omit information needed for correctness or hide uncertainty. This guide does not grant tools, permissions, or file access.
